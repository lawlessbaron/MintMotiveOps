GST Collected is calculated exactly, from paid/sent invoice lines in the period. GST Paid is summed straight from the Expenses ledger — PO receipts file themselves there automatically, and everything else (rent, software subscriptions, freight, tools, utilities, professional services) is added manually. It's only as complete as that ledger is kept, so treat this as a strong starting point for BAS prep, not a lodging-ready figure — check with your bookkeeper/accountant before lodging.

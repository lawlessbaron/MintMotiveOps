Moving to a new server

One file holds everything: every record in every table (parts and stock, sales and purchase orders, quotes, invoices, clients, suppliers, kits, builds, assets, documents, settings, users) and every uploaded image and document. Password-reset codes and the login-attempt log are left out.

  1. On the old server: Download full backup below.
  2. On the new server: sign in as an Owner, come to this page, and Restore the file.
  3. Sign in again with your usual account from the old server, check everything is there, then move your domain and Stripe webhook across.

Works between any two installs, SQLite or Postgres, Windows or Railway. For the Stripe and SMTP details saved in Integrations to carry over, give the new server the same SECRET_KEY as the old one.
